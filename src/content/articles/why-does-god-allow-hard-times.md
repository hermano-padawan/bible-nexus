The short answer is that the Bible does not give one explanation for every painful event. Some suffering comes from human choices, some from living in a broken world, and some remains a mystery to us.

## God is not cold toward pain

Jesus wept beside the grave of His friend Lazarus. That moment matters. God does not ask hurting people to pretend that loss is easy.

## Hard times are not proof that God has left

Jesus told His followers that trouble would come, and He also told them to take heart. Christian hope is not the claim that nothing bad will happen. It is trust that evil and pain will not have the final word.

You are allowed to ask why. You are allowed to grieve. Bring both your questions and your pain to God, and let trusted people walk beside you.
