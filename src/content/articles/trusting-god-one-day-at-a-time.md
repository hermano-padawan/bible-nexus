Tomorrow can feel very large when you are already tired today. Your mind runs ahead, trying to solve conversations that have not happened and problems that have not arrived.

Jesus brings us back to today. Not because tomorrow does not matter, but because grace meets us where our feet are.

Take one slow breath. Thank God for one good thing that is here now. Place one worry in His hands. Then do the next kind and faithful thing you know to do.

You do not need tomorrow's strength yet. Today, ask for today's bread, today's wisdom, and today's peace.
