Lord, today I feel alone. You know the quiet places in my heart and the words I have not been able to say.

Please stay close to me. Remind me that Your presence does not depend on what I feel in this moment. Bring the right people into my path, and give me courage to reach out instead of hiding my pain.

Guard my thoughts from hopelessness. Give me rest tonight and enough strength for tomorrow. Help me notice the small signs of Your care.

I place this lonely place in Your hands. Hold me here, and lead me forward one step at a time. Amen.
