Hard times can make everything feel uncertain. These references point to a steady truth: God remains near.

## When you need a safe place

Read **Psalm 46:1**. God is described as our refuge and strength, present when trouble comes.

## When fear is loud

Read **Isaiah 41:10**. The passage calls God's people away from fear because He is with them and will uphold them.

## When you wonder if anything can separate you from God's love

Read **Romans 8:38–39**. Paul names every kind of power and declares that none of them can separate us from God's love in Christ.

Read slowly. Choose one reference to return to today. Let it become a place where your thoughts can rest.
