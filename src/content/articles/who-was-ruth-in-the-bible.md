Ruth was a Moabite woman who became part of the people of Israel. Her story begins with grief. She lost her husband, and her mother-in-law Naomi had also suffered deep loss.

## A loyal choice

Ruth chose to remain with Naomi. She left her familiar home and walked toward an uncertain future. Her faith was not loud. It appeared in loyalty, work, kindness, and courage.

In Bethlehem, Ruth gathered leftover grain so that she and Naomi could eat. There she met Boaz, a man who treated her with dignity and became part of God's provision for their family.

## Why Ruth matters

Ruth's story shows that loss does not have to be the final chapter. God can work through ordinary decisions and faithful people. Ruth later became an ancestor of King David, placing this foreign widow within the family line that leads to Jesus.
