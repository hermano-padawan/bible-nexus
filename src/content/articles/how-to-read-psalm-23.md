Psalm 23 is short, but it speaks to many parts of life: need, rest, fear, danger, belonging, and hope.

## The Lord as shepherd

David knew what shepherds did. They guided, protected, searched, and stayed with the flock. Calling God a shepherd is a way of saying, “My life is in caring hands.”

## The dark valley

The psalm does not promise a life without valleys. It promises presence inside them. The path may be dark, but the shepherd has not disappeared.

## A table in the middle of trouble

The table shows welcome and provision even while enemies remain nearby. Peace does not always begin after every problem ends. God can meet us before the situation is resolved.

Read Psalm 23 slowly and notice which image you need today: rest, guidance, presence, welcome, or a home with God.
