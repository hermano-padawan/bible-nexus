**Redemption means rescue that brings someone into freedom.** In Bible times, the word could describe releasing a person from debt or slavery by paying a price.

## Redemption in the Bible's story

God described Israel's rescue from Egypt as redemption. He brought people out of slavery and made them His own.

In the New Testament, redemption points to what Jesus has done. Through Him, people receive forgiveness and freedom from sin.

## Why it matters

Redemption means your failures do not need to own your future. God's grace does more than cover the past. It brings you into a new life with Him.
